The Complete Rewear Cycle

We don't burn, shred, or downcycle. Every garment entrusted to Rewear moves through this transparent 6-stage circular loop from your wardrobe back into modern everyday life.

01 📦

Collect

Free doorstep courier pickup scheduled directly from your home across India. We collect your worn t-shirts, faded denim jeans, flannels, and cotton shirts.

Doorstep Collection
02 🔬

Sort

Smart optical fiber classification. Garments are graded by fabric density (GSM), tensile strength, cotton ratio, and curated color harmonies for aesthetic pairing.

AI Fiber Grading
03 ✨

Clean

100% waterless hospital-grade ozone sterilization & high-temperature steam deodorization. Destroys 99.9% of bacteria and allergens while saving thousands of liters of water.

Waterless Ozone
04 ✂️

Reuse

Preserving original craftsmanship details. Authentic denim pockets, heavy brass hardware, graphic cuts, and buttons are carefully unpicked and integrated intact.

Detail Preservation
05 🪡

Make

Master artisan tailoring on heritage handlooms. Hand-braided floor mats, quilted patchwork bedsheets, cushion covers, pet beds, and reinforced tote bags.

Artisan Handcrafted
06 🔄

Sell / Return

Your finished heirloom creation is delivered back to your doorstep, or certified artisan editions are cataloged for purchase in our circular community marketplace.

Doorstep Return & Market

ABOUT US • ZERO TEXTILE WASTE

The Rewear Philosophy: Old Clothes to Modern Life

Every home in our neighborhoods has clothes that sit unworn: t-shirts with faded graphic prints, jeans that wore through the knee, or shirts with missing buttons.

Rewear gives them a second life. We don't discard them or downcycle them into industrial stuffing. We deconstruct them by hand, deep-sanitize them in ozone chambers, and turn them into:

  • 1. Braided Floor Mats: Circular rugs hand-braided from cotton jersey ribbons that bring warmth to any room (Charge: ₹349).
  • 2. Patchwork Bed Sheets & Quilts: Soft double-bed quilts made from 8-10 cotton shirts and flannels (Charge: ₹899).
  • 3. Everyday Carry Bags: Durable tote bags and shopping bags that replace thousands of single-use plastic bags (Charge: ₹249 - ₹289).

All our tailoring charges are capped strictly between ₹200 and ₹1000 so that circular fashion is affordable for every single household.
